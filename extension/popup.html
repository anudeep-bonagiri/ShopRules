    Force full recheck ignores the "unchanged source" shortcut and sends every rule of the product on this tab to the model. It costs model calls; use it when you believe a source changed and the check said otherwise.

    How to use

    1. Open a product on Amazon, Etsy, or your Shopify store, or hover one in search results.
    2. Press Alt+Shift+S, or click the green button in the corner.
    3. Click Watch this product. You get notified when its price or its rules change.
