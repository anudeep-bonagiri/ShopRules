# Demo script, 3 minutes

Record at 1920x1080, browser zoom 110 percent, the extension loaded unpacked from `extension/dist`, the demo workspace seeded (Watchlist, "Load demo shop") and connected to the extension from Settings ("Connect Chrome extension"). Every spoken number is read off the screen. Say "Nemotron", "Nebius Token Factory", and "Tavily" while they are visible. No em dashes in captions.

| Time | Say | Do | See |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:20 | "Tariffs moved again this week. I sell forty listings on Etsy and Amazon and I cannot read the Federal Register every morning. Shop Rules watches the rules that touch my actual products, verifies every alert against the official text, and does the reprice math for me." | Hold on the Watchlist for two seconds, then switch to a real Etsy listing tab | Watchlist with the three metrics and the priority cards, then the Etsy page |
| 0:20 to 0:50 | "On any listing, hover and press Alt Shift S. Shop Rules reads the page, works out what it is, where it is from, and its tariff heading from a curated catalog, so the model cannot invent one." | Hover the product, press Alt+Shift+S, point at the header pills and the price line | The HUD slides in: marketplace pill, origin pill, title, price "read from page markup, verify" |
| 0:50 to 1:20 | "Watch it. Three steps: read the listing, classify, seed the rules. Now four tiles: price with its history, landed cost and margin, duty exposure with the official heading, weekly margin drag. Below, every rule that touches this product, grouped: tariffs, safety, sales tax, policy." | Click "Watch this product", wait for the three-step strip, scroll the tiles and the rules list | Progress strip, then the stats tiles and grouped rules with "Not checked" pills |
| 1:20 to 1:50 | "Check now. Tavily searches only the official domains for each rule, extracts the pages, and Nemotron Ultra on Nebius Token Factory decides whether it touches this product. A verifier outside the model drops any quote that is not in the text. Here is the alert, and here is the quote from ustr.gov." | Click "Check now", watch "Checked 2 of 4: ustr.gov", then click "Open evidence" on the alert | Live progress, then the alert page: evidence bar, the backed quote, the Source trail card listing the Tavily calls |
| 1:50 to 2:10 | "The price goes up, not down. A 12.5 point duty increase on a lamp that costs me 13.50 landed adds 1.43 a unit. Holding my 55 percent margin means 33 dollars, not 29.99. That math is deterministic; the model only reports the facts, and the guardrails list anything they changed." | Point at the reprice block and the one-sentence explanation, then at "Guardrails adjusted this verdict" if present | Recommended reprice, "Preserves 55% margin", the explanation sentence |
| 2:10 to 2:30 | "Prices are tracked. The extension posts the price every time I visit, the daily refresh reads the listing with Tavily Extract and no model, and a move past my threshold is an alert with a notification." | Open the Inspector, point at the change and sparkline on a row, open a product and switch the chart range | Inspector rows with change since added, the price chart with range tabs, high and low |
| 2:30 to 2:50 | "Nexus thresholds for the states I ship to, verified on the revenue sites with Tavily Map and Extract, both tests, and who collects. And the receipt: what Tavily read, what each model cost, and what routing saved." | Watchlist nexus section, click "Verify on the revenue sites", then Cost receipt | Nexus cards with "verified today on comptroller.texas.gov", then the Tavily and model receipts |
| 2:50 to 3:00 | "What is not built: no store write-back, no login, seeds stay labelled seeds until a page confirms them. It is all in the not-built list in the repo." | Show `docs/not-built.md` in the repo | The honest list |

Fallbacks: if a live check returns "nothing new", say so and open an existing alert instead; never narrate a number the screen does not show. If the extension cannot connect, paste the token from Settings into the popup.
