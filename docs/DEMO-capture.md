# Capturing the extension loop (20 seconds)

The Devpost video needs one continuous take of hover, inspect, watch, stats. This is the exact click path, timed, with what must be visible in each beat. Stills of every state are in `docs/design/shots/ext-*.png` (generated by `node extension/test/shots.mts`).

## Setup (before recording)

1. `npm run build:ext`, load `extension/dist` unpacked at `chrome://extensions`, pin the icon.
2. Open the Shop Rules app, Settings, click **Connect Chrome extension**. The page says "Connected to extension"; the popup badge says Connected.
3. Make sure the demo product is **not** yet watched (delete it from the Inspector if it is), so the Watch button shows.
4. Open a real Etsy search page (for example `etsy.com/search?q=wooden+rainbow+stacker`) in a 1280 px wide window. Screen recorder at 30 fps, cursor visible.

## The take

| Time | Do | What the viewer sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Hold **Alt**, move the cursor over one listing card | A green outline snaps to the card, pill reads "Alt+Shift+S to inspect" |
| 0:03 | Press **Alt+Shift+S** | The HUD slides in from the right: Etsy pill, title, price with "read from page markup, verify", primary **Watch this product** button, four stat tiles (price, landed cost estimate, duty exposure "Classified when you watch it", weekly drag) |
| 0:06 | Click **Watch this product** | The three-step strip appears under the header: Reading listing, Classifying, Seeding rules, each turning green in turn (20 to 40 s on a real page; cut to 0:12 in the edit) |
| 0:12 | Wait for the pill "Watching since today" | Tiles fill: HS heading from the catalog, origin, the rules card lists Tariffs, Safety, Sales tax, Policy with "Not checked" pills |
| 0:15 | Click **Check now** | "Checked 2 of 9: ustr.gov, cpsc.gov" with a progress bar; rule pills flip to Checking (pulsing) then Clear or Alert |
| 0:19 | Scroll the HUD to the top alert | Severity pill, headline, evidence bar, metrics, and the reprice block with a price that is higher than the current one and a one-sentence explanation |
| 0:20 | Stop | |

## Second take, 10 seconds (price change)

1. In Settings, set the price-change threshold to 1%.
2. On the watched product's page, edit the price in the store (or on a test Shopify store) and reload the listing with the extension active.
3. Within a second the content script posts the price; the badge count goes up and a Chrome notification shows the product title and "... is now $27.49, down 8.3% from $29.99".
4. Click the notification: the alert page opens with Was / Now and the recomputed margin.
